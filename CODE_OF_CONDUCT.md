# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in CrowdTensor a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and prioritize community safety.
- Keep credentials, private endpoints, personal data, and exploit details out
  of public issues and discussions.
- Respect platform resource policies and the owners of contributed compute.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, publishing private information,
and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues through the repository's private security or maintainer
contact path. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct is inappropriate. Reports will
be handled as privately as practical.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
